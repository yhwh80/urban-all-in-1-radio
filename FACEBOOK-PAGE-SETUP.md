## ⚠️ Before you post anything with music in it

**PPL and PRS cover broadcasting only.** They do **not** cover putting recorded music into
social posts — that is a separate sync/social licence, and Facebook's automated system
will flag or mute it regardless of what the station is licensed for.

Safe to post: talk-only clips, the AI DJ's own generated voice, your own graphics and
artwork, Facebook's own licensed audio library, and anything an artist has given you
direct written permission to use.

Not safe: clips of the stream, or any post with a commercial recording under it.

## After the Page exists

1. **Set the username** — Settings → Page setup → Username. Do this early; it's what
   makes the Page findable and gives you a clean `facebook.com/ua1radio` link.
2. **Add the website link** to the Page's intro.
3. **Update the site's social links** — `website/README.md` still has placeholder
   `instagram.com/yourpage` / `twitter.com/yourpage`. The live pages don't show them
   yet, so adding real icons to the site is a separate small job.
4. **Meta Business Suite** — only worth connecting when you actually want to run ads or
   give someone else access. Not needed to run the Page.

## Worth doing before/alongside this

Per the marketing notes, the station's bottleneck is **discovery, not content**. A Facebook
Page with no followers reaches nobody on its own. The higher-value action is the radio
directory submissions — TuneIn, Radio Garden, Online Radio Box, Streema, myTuner — because
people actively browse those looking for stations. Prep is ready in
`radio-directory-submission-info.txt` (stream URL in it was corrected 2026-10-19; the old
one pointed at the dead Coolify host).
